About protective stops & targets, and what the columns mean

When a buy fills, Claire leaves two orders at the broker to guard the shares — they work even when Claire, this PC or the internet is down:

  • 🛑 Stop-loss — sells the shares if the price falls to the stop price, so a loss cannot keep growing. Once the stop price is hit it sells at the market, so the fill can be a little below it (CSCO's 96.97 stop filled at 96.90).
  • 🎯 Take-profit — sells the shares if the price rises to the target, locking in the gain. It never sells below the target.
  • They are a pair: when one fills, the broker cancels the other.

Shares how many it would sell · Sells at the stop or target price · Current price the latest price and how far it has to move to reach the order · You paid your buy price · If it fills profit or loss on those shares versus what you paid, before fees · Value shares × the order price.

To change them, sell the position (Portfolio, Saxo accounts or Watchlist — that cancels them first) or change them on Saxo's platform.
